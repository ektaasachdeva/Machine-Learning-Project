Problem Statement & Sociotechnical Context

Automated classification pipelines deployed in high-stakes socioeconomic decision processes—including automated recruitment screening, credit risk underwriting, and public entitlement scoring—often inherit, institutionalize, and amplify historical societal inequalities.

When an algorithm is trained to predict whether an individual's annual income exceeds $50,000 using the Adult Census Dataset, it optimizes solely for accuracy against historical 1994 data. Because women historically faced occupational segregation and unequal caregiving burdens, an unmitigated model learns to view gender as an eligibility proxy. This projects historical wage disparities into the future under the guise of mathematical neutrality.

Core Research Objective: Benchmark 4 machine learning classifiers (Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Gaussian Naive Bayes, and k-NN), audit their error rates across gender groups, prove why removing gender fails (proxy encoding), and implement Kamiran & Calders sample re-weighting to restore fairness without destroying predictive utility.

SDG 5: Gender Equality

Target 5.1 & 5.5: End all forms of discrimination against women and ensure equal leadership opportunities.

Unmitigated ML models exhibit a False Negative Rate of 43.1% for females versus 36.8% for males. In automated recruitment and loan applications, qualified high-earning women are disproportionately rejected. This project eliminates that asymmetric error barrier.

SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

Target 10.2 & 10.3: Promote social and economic inclusion of all and ensure equal opportunity by reducing inequalities of outcome.

Pre-processing sample re-weighting mathematically balances the joint probability of gender and income ($P(S, Y)$), decoupling credit scoring models from historical marginalization with less than a $1.2\%$ accuracy loss.

Why Logistic Regression Wins on Tabular Data

L2 weight regularization ($C=1.0$) combined with standard feature scaling provides smooth probability estimates and prevents single continuous variables (like capital gains) from dominating predictions. It attains both the top F1-score (0.655) and highest ranking discrimination (ROC-AUC 0.905).

The Failure Mode of Naive Bayes

Gaussian Naive Bayes suffers from severe False Positive inflation due to violation of conditional feature independence in census data (education, age, and occupation are heavily intercorrelated), depressing precision to 0.563.

Analysis: Unmitigated Baseline Classifier

The baseline Logistic Regression achieves the highest raw predictive accuracy (85.12%) and F1-score (0.655). However, it exhibits severe disparate impact (0.310), accepting only 7.01% of women versus 22.58% of men. This violates the legal 4/5ths standard.
